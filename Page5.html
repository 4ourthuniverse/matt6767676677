The World of Jazz

Jazz is a genre of music known for its smooth rhythms, expressive melodies, improvisation, and rich harmonies. It often features instruments such as the saxophone, trumpet, piano, double bass, and drums. Originating in the African American communities of New Orleans in the late 19th and early 20th centuries, jazz has evolved into many styles, including swing, bebop, cool jazz, and fusion. It is often associated with a relaxed, sophisticated, and soulful atmosphere.

Jazz allows musicians to express their emotions and creativity through music.
